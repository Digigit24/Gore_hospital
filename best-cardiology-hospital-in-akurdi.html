About Gore Multispeciality Hospital

At Gore Multispeciality Hospital, we believe that excellent healthcare is not just about advanced machines and medicines—it’s also about care, respect, and understanding. Recognised as a leading multispeciality hospital, we are proud to be a trusted healthcare partner for families across the region.

Our hospital was built with a clear vision: to bring high-quality medical services under one roof, close to home. Today, we offer advanced treatments across multiple specialities, supported by modern diagnostic facilities and 24/7 emergency care.

Our Heart Services Under One Roof

1. Heart Checkups & Tests

Keeping your heart healthy starts with regular checkups. At Gore Multispeciality Hospital, we offer ECG, ECHO, stress tests, and Holter monitoring to make sure your heart is in top shape. It’s simple, thorough, and gives you peace of mind about your heart’s health.

Learn More

2. Interventional Cardiology

If there’s a blockage or a rhythm issue, our team is ready to help. We perform advanced procedures like angioplasty, stent placement, and pacemaker implantations. We use minimally invasive techniques so you can recover faster and get back to your life.

Learn More

3. Heart Surgeries

For complex heart conditions, our expert surgeons provide life-saving treatments like CABG (bypass surgery) and valve repairs. We combine advanced technology with a personal touch to ensure the best possible outcome for you and your family.

Learn More

4. Preventive Cardiology

The best way to treat a heart problem is to stop it before it starts. We provide guided lifestyle changes, diet plans, and risk assessments to help you manage blood pressure and cholesterol, keeping your heart strong for years to come.

Learn More

5. Emergency Cardiac Care

Heart emergencies don’t wait, and neither do we. Our 24/7 cardiac emergency unit is always ready to handle chest pain, heart attacks, and other critical situations. With our dedicated Cardiac ICU, you’re in safe hands every second.

Frequently Asked Questions

The best cardiology hospital is one that has experienced heart doctors, modern testing facilities, and emergency care available at all times. At Gore Multispeciality Hospital, the focus is also on patient comfort and clear communication, so people understand their condition and treatment without confusion.

Doctors usually suggest tests like ECG, 2D Echo, TMT, and blood tests to understand how your heart is working. These tests help find blockages, rhythm problems, or weak heart muscles and guide the doctor in choosing the right treatment.

Chest pain is not always caused by heart problems. It can also happen due to gas, muscle strain, or anxiety. However, since heart-related chest pain can be dangerous, it is always safer to consult a doctor and get proper evaluation.

Angiography is a special test that helps doctors see if there are blockages in the heart’s blood vessels. It is done when a person has chest pain or other symptoms of heart disease, so doctors can decide the best treatment option.

Yes, emergency heart care is available round the clock for patients with chest pain, heart attack symptoms, or sudden breathing difficulty. Quick testing and treatment during an emergency can save lives, which is why emergency cardiac care is very important.

Common heart attack symptoms include chest pain or pressure, pain in the left arm or jaw, shortness of breath, sweating, nausea, and sudden weakness. Some people may also feel dizziness or extreme tiredness. Any of these signs need immediate medical attention.

Heart blockage usually happens when cholesterol and fat build up inside the blood vessels over many years. This is often linked to unhealthy eating, lack of exercise, smoking, obesity, diabetes, and stress. Over time, this buildup reduces blood flow to the heart.

Yes, heart problems can affect young people too. Stress, poor diet, smoking, lack of exercise, obesity, and family history can increase risk even at a young age. That is why young adults should also pay attention to heart health and lifestyle habits.
